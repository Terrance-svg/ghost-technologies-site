The app may show the applicable restore-through date. If paid access is restored before that retention period expires, the retained backup may be preserved and normal eligible cloud access can resume. After the retention period expires, the retained backup is no longer available for restore and its encrypted cloud snapshot and associated cloud attachments are queued for deletion through Ghost’s retention-purge process. Resubscribing after the retention period has expired does not make the expired backup restorable again. The user’s local vault on their device is not deleted by this cloud-retention process.

Account deletion can remove Ghost Cloud data earlier than the 90-day downgrade period. Limited billing, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, or legal records may be retained where required or reasonably necessary.

6. AI Scan, Batch AI, Document Organizer, and Walkthrough AI

AI-assisted features are optional. AI Scan analyzes an item image the signed-in user deliberately submits. Batch AI can analyze up to 5 selected item photos as separate scans. Document Organizer can analyze up to 5 selected receipt or document files at a time; in this release the first page of a PDF is used for AI analysis. Walkthrough AI analyzes selected still views extracted locally from a room recording rather than sending the full temporary AI recording as the normal analysis payload.

Walkthrough AI credit accounting is item-oriented: a usable distinct item can consume one scan credit, while empty or unclear views, automatic retry, and bounded value verification do not add an extra credit for that same request. Standard Photo Scan and Document Organizer use the scan allowance shown in the app for each analyzed item image or document.

AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated. Suggested names, categories, brands, models, serial information, conditions, document matches, confidence, and replacement-value guidance must be reviewed before saving or confirming changes. AI guidance is not a professional appraisal, insurance valuation, coverage decision, or promise of reimbursement.

9. Recovery Passphrase and Encrypted Vault Access

The recovery passphrase protects access to the encrypted private-vault backup. Ghost Inventory does not store the plaintext recovery passphrase and cannot retrieve or display it for the user.

The user is responsible for securely saving the new recovery passphrase before leaving the replacement flow. If the current passphrase is lost and no usable unlocked vault or trusted unlocked device remains, encrypted cloud content may be permanently inaccessible. Ghost cannot bypass the encryption or reset access from the account password alone.
