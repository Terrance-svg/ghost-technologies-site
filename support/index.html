Common questions

Answers before you wait for a reply.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Google Play, select your profile, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Ghost Inventory. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Why is Ghost Cloud unavailable on Free?

The Free plan stores inventory locally on the device. Encrypted Ghost Cloud backup and restore are included with Premium and Pro / Estate.

What happens if I downgrade?

Your local vault is not deleted because of a paid-to-Free downgrade. Existing saved records remain available, while new activity can be limited by the Free plan. New Ghost Cloud backups pause, and an eligible encrypted backup may remain restorable for up to 90 days before expired cloud data is queued for deletion.

Can support see my inventory?

Ghost Cloud is designed around encrypted private-vault backups. Support should not request or automatically receive your inventory content, photos, receipts, addresses or recovery keys during normal troubleshooting.

Can Ghost recover a lost vault passphrase?

Ghost cannot retrieve a plaintext recovery passphrase. From an already unlocked vault or trusted unlocked device, you can replace it after re-authentication; the new passphrase invalidates the previous one. Without the valid passphrase or a usable unlocked trusted device, the encrypted backup may be permanently inaccessible.

What is included in a Full Evidence Report?

The Android launch build is designed to include an inventory summary plus readable selected item photos, receipt images and PDF pages, warranties, appraisals, certificates, ownership records, repair estimates and serial or VIN proof. Reports organize user-provided records and do not guarantee insurer acceptance or claim approval.

How do I delete my account?

Use Settings → Account → Delete Account in Ghost Inventory, or follow the instructions on the Delete Account page.

How many AI scans are included?

Free includes 3 AI scans per month, Premium includes 150, and Pro / Estate includes 400. Separate one-time AI top-ups are also available through Google Play. Monthly plan scans are used before non-expiring top-up scans; the app shows the live remaining balance for the signed-in account.

How does Walkthrough AI use credits?

A usable distinct item found by Walkthrough AI can consume one AI scan credit. Empty or unclear views, automatic retry, and bounded value verification do not add another credit for that same request.
