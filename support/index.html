Ghost Inventory Support

Need help with your account, recovery, AI scans, billing, reports or Ghost Cloud? Start here.

Email supportOpen Google Play

Account & sign-in

For confirmation, password reset or Google sign-in issues, include the app version and a brief description. Never send your password or recovery passphrase.

AI scans & documents

AI results are editable guidance. If a scan fails, tell us which AI flow you used and what message appeared. Avoid emailing private vault content unless you intentionally choose to attach it.

Subscriptions & credits

Google Play manages purchases, renewals, cancellations and payment methods. Ghost verifies purchases before activating paid access or top-up credits.

Ghost Cloud & recovery

Ghost cannot retrieve or reset a plaintext recovery passphrase. Restore requires an available encrypted backup plus the current recovery passphrase and account access.

When you contact support

Please include what you were trying to do, what happened, and the Ghost Inventory version shown in the app. Screenshots are optional and may contain private information, so review them before sending.
