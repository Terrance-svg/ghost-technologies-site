Straight answers

Know how Ghost works before you trust it.

Privacy, AI, subscriptions and recovery deserve clear language.

Can Ghost Inventory read my private inventory?

Ghost Inventory is designed to minimize readable cloud data. Sensitive paid-plan vault backups are encrypted on the device before upload, and Ghost Inventory does not store the plaintext recovery passphrase or a server-side key intended to decrypt that private-vault backup. Limited operational metadata such as account status, plan, usage and synchronization timestamps is still used to operate the service.

Are AI value suggestions professional appraisals?

No. AI-assisted item details and replacement-value guidance can be incomplete or inaccurate and must be reviewed before saving. They are not professional appraisals, insurance valuations, coverage decisions or guarantees of reimbursement.

What happens if I cancel or downgrade?

A normal Google Play cancellation stops future renewal while paid access continues through the applicable paid period. When the account returns to Free, the local vault is not deleted. New Ghost Cloud backups pause, and an eligible encrypted backup may remain restorable for up to 90 days before expired cloud data is queued for deletion.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Eligible paid users can restore an available encrypted Ghost Cloud backup on a supported device using the current recovery passphrase and account access. Ghost cannot retrieve the plaintext recovery passphrase, so it should be stored somewhere safe outside the phone.

How does Walkthrough AI use scan credits?

A usable distinct item can consume one AI scan credit. Empty or unclear views, automatic retry, and bounded value verification do not add an extra credit for that same request.

Does deleting my Ghost account cancel Google Play billing?

No. Account deletion and Google Play subscription cancellation are separate actions. A subscription must be cancelled through Google Play to stop future renewals.
