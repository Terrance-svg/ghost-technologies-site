STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Know what Ghost does—and what it does not.

Can Ghost Inventory read my private inventory?

Ghost Inventory is designed to minimize readable cloud data. Sensitive paid-plan vault backups are encrypted on the device before upload, and Ghost Inventory does not store the plaintext recovery passphrase or a server-side key intended to decrypt that private-vault backup.

Are AI value suggestions professional appraisals?

No. AI-assisted item details and replacement-value guidance can be incomplete or inaccurate and must be reviewed before saving. They are not professional appraisals, insurance valuations, coverage decisions or guarantees of reimbursement.

What happens if I cancel or downgrade?

A normal Google Play cancellation stops future renewal while paid access continues through the applicable paid period. When the account returns to Free, the local vault is not deleted. New Ghost Cloud backups pause.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Eligible paid users can restore an available encrypted Ghost Cloud backup on a supported device using the current recovery passphrase and account access. Ghost cannot retrieve the plaintext recovery passphrase.
