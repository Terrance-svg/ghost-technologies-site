ACCOUNT & DATA

Delete your Ghost Account

Ghost Inventory provides an in-app account-deletion flow. You can also contact support if you cannot access the app.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Ghost Inventory and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to Help, Privacy & Sharing → Account & Data Deletion.
  4. Review the deletion information and complete the confirmation steps.

If you cannot access the app

Send an account-deletion request from the email address associated with your Ghost Account. Do not send your password or recovery passphrase.

Request Account Deletion

What deletion is intended to remove

Account deletion is intended to remove the Ghost Account, cloud profile, encrypted snapshots, and supported cloud attachments associated with the account, subject to required legal, security, fraud-prevention, billing, and backup retention.

Important: Google Play subscription

Deleting a Ghost Account does not automatically cancel Google Play billing. Cancel the subscription separately through Google Play.

Manage Google Play subscriptions
Local vault data may remain on a device until the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. If you still control the device, remove local data separately if that is what you want.
