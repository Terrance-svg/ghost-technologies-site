PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

How Ghost Inventory handles account information, encrypted vault content, billing, AI-assisted analysis, support and service diagnostics.

Effective July 26, 2026Last updated July 26, 2026Version 2026-07-26

This Privacy Policy explains how Ghost Inventory (“Ghost,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Ghost Inventory, Ghost Cloud, the Ghost Inventory website, support, reports, subscriptions, AI-assisted features, and related services.

Ghost Inventory is designed around data minimization and client-side encryption. The app owner and ordinary Ghost administrators are not intended to be able to read supported encrypted vault content stored in Ghost Cloud.

1. Information We Handle

Account and authentication information

We may process your account identifier, email address, authentication-provider identifiers, confirmation status, session information, account creation time, last sign-in time, and security events. Password authentication is handled by our authentication provider. Ghost does not receive your plaintext account password.

Encrypted vault content

Supported sensitive vault content is encrypted on your device before Ghost Cloud upload. This may include property and room information, item details, notes, recorded values, serial numbers, photographs, receipts, warranties, supporting documents, and encrypted backup snapshots. Ghost Cloud stores ciphertext and the recovery-wrapped vault-key envelope. Ghost does not store the plaintext recovery passphrase or the server-side key needed to independently decrypt the vault.

Operational metadata

To operate the Service, we may process limited metadata that is not protected by the vault’s content-encryption key, such as account status, subscription plan, AI-credit usage, report-allowance usage, encrypted-storage usage, backup timestamps, app version, encryption version, synchronization status, product identifiers, purchase-verification status, and technical request status.

Billing information

Google Play processes payment-card and purchase transactions. Ghost may receive or store Google Play product IDs, base-plan IDs, purchase tokens, subscription state, acknowledgement state, start and expiry times, and verification responses needed to activate and maintain plan access. Ghost does not receive your full payment-card number from Google Play.

AI-assisted analysis information

AI Scan and AI-assisted Walkthrough analysis operate only after you intentionally select or capture media in those flows and proceed with analysis. The selected image or extracted key frame is decrypted or prepared on your device and transmitted through Ghost Cloud to an AI processing provider, currently OpenAI, for item analysis. The service may process visible objects, text, brand, model, condition, category, and value-related context to return an editable result. Ghost applies usage controls and encrypts supported structured AI results for delivery back to the app. Do not submit images containing unrelated people, highly sensitive personal information, or content you do not have permission to process.

Support and communications

When you contact support, we process the email address, message, attachments you choose to include, device/app diagnostics included in the draft, and related correspondence. Ghost does not automatically attach your private vault or documents to a support email.

Website and service diagnostics

Our website and service providers may process IP address, browser or device type, request timestamps, security logs, error logs, and similar diagnostic information. Safe billing diagnostics are designed not to log purchase tokens, access tokens, private keys, authorization headers, or service-account JSON.

2. Recovery Passphrase and Encrypted Vault Access

Your Ghost Account password and recovery passphrase serve different purposes. The account password authenticates your Ghost Account. The separate recovery passphrase protects the encrypted vault key used to restore a supported Ghost Cloud backup on another device.

The recovery passphrase is created and processed on your device. Ghost does not receive or retain the plaintext passphrase and cannot retrieve, display, or reset it.

You may generate a replacement passphrase only from an already unlocked vault or trusted unlocked device after re-authentication. When replacement succeeds, the prior passphrase is invalidated. You are responsible for securely saving the replacement.

If you lose the recovery passphrase and every trusted unlocked device, or if no successful encrypted Ghost Cloud backup exists, encrypted content may be permanently inaccessible.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • create and authenticate accounts;
  • provide local vault, encrypted backup, restore, report, and claim-preparation features;
  • verify Google Play subscriptions and apply plan limits;
  • process user-initiated AI analysis;
  • maintain AI credits and shared monthly report allowances;
  • provide support and service communications;
  • protect accounts, detect fraud, enforce limits, and investigate failures;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Service;
  • comply with legal obligations.

Optional marketing messages are sent only where you separately consent or another lawful basis permits them. Marketing consent is not required to use Ghost Inventory and can be withdrawn.

4. When Information Is Shared

We may share limited information with service providers only as necessary to operate the Service, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and Edge Functions;
  • Google Play for subscriptions, purchase verification, and app distribution;
  • OpenAI for user-initiated AI image analysis;
  • Resend and recipient email providers for authentication and service emails;
  • hosting, security, analytics, and support providers used by the Ghost Inventory website or operations;
  • authorities or professional advisers where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, and legal compliance.

We do not sell users’ personal information or private vault content to advertisers. Advertisers do not receive Ghost Inventory conversations or encrypted vault contents from us.

5. Storage, Security, and International Processing

Ghost uses safeguards that may include AES-256-GCM client-side vault encryption, Android Keystore protection for device access, private storage buckets, row-level security, authenticated server functions, purchase verification, transport encryption, access restrictions, and data minimization.

No security system is perfect. Device compromise, weak account security, lost recovery material, malicious software, provider outages, user error, or vulnerabilities can affect confidentiality or availability.

Service providers may process information in Canada, the United States, or other countries where they operate. Privacy laws in those locations may differ from those in your province or country.

6. Retention

We retain account, operational, subscription, and support information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and enforce agreements.

Encrypted cloud snapshots and attachments remain until replaced, deleted through supported features, or removed during account deletion, subject to backup, security, and legal-retention periods. AI request material is handled according to Ghost’s configuration and the applicable processor terms; Ghost does not use private vault content for advertising.

Local vault data remains on a device until deleted in the app, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Deleting the cloud account does not automatically erase files remaining on a user-controlled device.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain information about personal information Ghost controls, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You can:

  • update supported account and vault information in the app;
  • disable optional biometric unlock or cloud backup;
  • withdraw optional marketing consent;
  • delete supported records or attachments;
  • request account deletion through the app or published deletion page;
  • cancel a subscription through Google Play.

Because Ghost does not possess the plaintext recovery passphrase or vault decryption key, Ghost may be technically unable to provide readable copies of encrypted content that only you can decrypt.

8. Account Deletion

Account deletion is intended to remove the Ghost Account, cloud profile, encrypted snapshots, and supported cloud attachments associated with the account, subject to required legal, security, fraud-prevention, billing, and backup retention.

Deleting a Ghost Account does not automatically cancel Google Play billing. Cancel the subscription separately in Google Play. Local data may remain on a device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

9. Children

Ghost Inventory is not directed to children who cannot legally consent to the processing described in this Policy. A parent or legal guardian should supervise use where required by law. Do not submit information about a child unless you have lawful authority and it is necessary for a legitimate inventory record.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service changes. Material updates may require renewed in-app acknowledgement before entering the real vault or Demo Mode. The effective date and version shown in the app identify the policy accepted on that device.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to support@ghost-technologies.net.
